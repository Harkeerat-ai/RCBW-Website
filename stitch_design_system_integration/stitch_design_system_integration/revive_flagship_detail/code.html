Revive

A holistic approach to urban environmental sustainability. Project Revive focuses on breathing life back into our city's green spaces and coastal areas through direct action and community education.

The Mission

As urban landscapes expand, the vital ecosystems that support our city are under constant threat. Project Revive is our flagship environmental initiative designed to counteract this degradation through localized, high-impact interventions.

We believe that true sustainability starts at the grassroots level. By mobilizing youth volunteers, partnering with local authorities, and utilizing scientific planting methods, we aim to create self-sustaining green pockets and cleaner public spaces that benefit all residents.

Rooted in Change: Tree Plantation Drives

Our plantation drives go beyond merely putting saplings in the ground. We utilize the Miyawaki method to create dense, native forests that grow 10x faster and are 30x more dense than conventional plantations. These micro-forests become vital carbon sinks and biodiversity hotspots within the urban sprawl.

Restoring Shores: Coastal Clean-ups

Mumbai's coastline is integral to its identity, yet severely impacted by marine debris. Through systematic, organized clean-up drives, we actively remove tons of plastic and waste from our beaches and mangroves, while simultaneously campaigning for responsible waste disposal at the source.

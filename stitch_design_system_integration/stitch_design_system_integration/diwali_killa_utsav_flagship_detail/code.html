A celebration of cultural preservation through the traditional art of community fort-making, bridging generations and reviving historic practices in modern times.

The Legacy of Mud Forts

The tradition of building miniature mud forts (Killas) during Diwali is a cherished cultural practice in Maharashtra, honoring the legacy of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. Our initiative seeks to keep this hands-on heritage alive among urban youth.

By engaging children and families in the collaborative process of shaping mud, stones, and water into intricate fort replicas, we foster teamwork, creativity, and a tangible connection to regional history.

The Diwali Killa Utsav is not just an event; it's an immersive educational experience that transforms historical narratives into interactive community art.
